Help & FAQs

Find answers to common questions about using RemovePDFPages free PDF tools, Full Editor subscriptions, one-time license, pricing, privacy, and refunds.

Using tools

Do I need to create an account?
No. The free tools work without signup. The Full Editor is tied to a license key sent to your email.
Do the free tools add a watermark?
No. The free PDF tools do not add watermarks to your files.
Are my files uploaded to your server?
Remove Pages, Merge, Compress, and Sign run in your browser by default, so your files stay on your device. Convert to Word uses a backend server; files are uploaded temporarily and deleted within 1 hour.
Why does Convert to Word need a server?
PDF to Word conversion is too complex to run reliably in a browser. We upload the file temporarily, convert it, and delete it within 1 hour.
What are the file limits?
Each file can be up to 50 MB and 200 pages. Free tools have a fair-use limit of 10–20 tasks per hour from the same IP. Merge supports up to 10 files at once.

Privacy & security

Do you store my PDFs?
We do not store PDFs for the free tools. For Convert to Word and backend fallback, files are kept only for the time needed to process them and deleted automatically within 1 hour.
Do you share my data with third parties?
We do not sell or share your PDFs. We use Creem for payments (Merchant of Record), Resend for license emails, and Cloudflare for hosting. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Do you use analytics or cookies?
We may use analytics to improve the site. The specific tool and cookie use will be disclosed in our Privacy Policy once selected.

Pricing & license

Is the Full Editor a subscription?
We offer both subscription plans and a one-time license. The $19/month Launch Special and the $99/year plan are subscriptions. The $59 one-time license is a single payment with no recurring billing.
What does “one-time license” mean?
It means you pay once for the current major version of RemovePDFPages Full Editor (v1.x). It includes updates within that version, but a future new platform may require a separate license.
Can I buy at $19 later?
The $19/month Launch Special is available for a limited time and may end without notice. Once it ends, the standard monthly price will be $29 and the standard yearly price will be $149.
How many devices can I use?
One license or subscription can be activated on up to 5 personal devices. We record a device fingerprint during activation to enforce this limit.
How many Convert to Word conversions do I get?
Free users can try 3 conversions per 30-day period. Paid plans include 10 conversions per calendar month. Additional conversions are $0.50 each or $5 for 10.
What happens when the launch period ends?
We will return to the standard price of $29/month and $149/year. The $19/month Launch Special is limited time and may end without notice.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. Monthly and yearly subscriptions can be canceled anytime. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. Yearly plans are also eligible for a 14-day refund.

Top-up credits

How do top-up credits work?
After you use your 10 included Convert to Word conversions each month, you can buy extra credits at $0.50 each or $5 for 10. Unused credits are refundable within 14 days; used credits are not.
Can I refund top-up credits?
Unused credits are refundable within 14 days of purchase. Once a credit has been used for a conversion, it is not refundable.

Refunds

Can I get a refund?
Yes. We offer a 14-day refund policy for the Full Editor and unused top-up credits within 14 days of purchase. Contact us through the refund page or support email with your order information. Used credits are not refundable.
Are there any refund fees?
Payment processing fees are not refunded by Creem when a refund is issued. We absorb this cost per refund.
Can I refund after using the license?
We reserve the right to refuse refunds in cases of abuse, license key redistribution, or fraudulent purchases.

Sales tax & jurisdiction

Will I be charged sales tax or VAT?
For purchases in the United States, applicable state and local sales tax will be calculated and collected automatically by Creem at checkout based on your billing address. International purchases may be subject to VAT or other local taxes; the buyer is responsible for compliance with local laws.
